# Baibai — online virtual temple demo

`baibai.hk` — 拜拜 線上虛擬廟宇 Demo (static site, v35).

- Static, no build step, no server, no secrets. Entry point: `index.html`.
- Assets live under `assets/` (Heart Sutra chant audio, logo badge, three.js).
- Public version was v26; this repo deploys v33 (v30 bigger garden + faster walking, v31 lake lotus/flowers/dragonflies/butterflies/koi, v32 entry wording, v33: incense — 3 sticks per visit + smoke only after offering + burner sized to the figure; leaving the hall goes straight back to the courtyard; map doubled, lake enlarged, pond life random, taller trees and mountains, temple pushed back for depth, hall seats widened, entry page logo cleaned up, temple plaque stays Chinese in every language; v34: the visitor is re-skinned as a little Taoist acolyte （道童） — dark-blue robe, pale-gold sash, topknot, chubbier head — with a boy/girl acolyte picker on the entry screen; the designed 道童 art is used as the picker portraits and as the sweeping acolyte who greets you in the garden; courtyard gate and hall differentiated — gate taller with a vermilion-red roof, hall pushed back to z -54, scaled 0.85, deep-green roof, so the near building reads clearly bigger and the far one smaller, and the hall roofline no longer cuts across the gate roof; lake and lotus buttons fixed — the U+1FAB7 lotus emoji (Unicode 14, unreadable on older phones) replaced with a water wave and a cherry blossom (Unicode 6, on every phone); v35: directional arrow buttons (D-pad) in the garden and the hall — four small arrows (▲▼◀▶, old geometric glyphs, no emoji), shown only while the figure is walking and hidden when idle, the arrow matching the figure's current heading highlighted, hold to steer and release to stop; straight-line walking — the "go to the old tree" destination now stops right under the old pine instead of four units short, a collision dead zone that could leave a tap on a trunk centre circling for ever is fixed, and the garden camera is widened (FOV 60→66) and brought closer (follow 3.9→3.3) so a target grows clearly as you approach it; tree density — the courtyard trees are thinned from 39 to 34 (two centre-north outer trees and two of the five mid-ground trees behind the hall removed, one inner pine west of the path removed) with heights varied, so the tree line reads open and calm, not a dense wall.
- Deployed on Vercel (project `baibai`) with custom domains `baibai.hk` and `www.baibai.hk`.
