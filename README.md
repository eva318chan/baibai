# Baibai — online virtual temple demo

`baibai.hk` — 拜拜 線上虛擬廟宇 Demo (static site, v40).

- Static, no build step, no server, no secrets. Entry point: `index.html`.
- Assets live under `assets/` (Heart Sutra chant audio, logo badge, three.js).
- Public version was v26; this repo deploys v33 (v30 bigger garden + faster walking, v31 lake lotus/flowers/dragonflies/butterflies/koi, v32 entry wording, v33: incense — 3 sticks per visit + smoke only after offering + burner sized to the figure; leaving the hall goes straight back to the courtyard; map doubled, lake enlarged, pond life random, taller trees and mountains, temple pushed back for depth, hall seats widened, entry page logo cleaned up, temple plaque stays Chinese in every language; v34: the visitor is re-skinned as a little Taoist acolyte （道童） — dark-blue robe, pale-gold sash, topknot, chubbier head — with a boy/girl acolyte picker on the entry screen; the designed 道童 art is used as the picker portraits and as the sweeping acolyte who greets you in the garden; courtyard gate and hall differentiated — gate taller with a vermilion-red roof, hall pushed back to z -54, scaled 0.85, deep-green roof, so the near building reads clearly bigger and the far one smaller, and the hall roofline no longer cuts across the gate roof; lake and lotus buttons fixed — the U+1FAB7 lotus emoji (Unicode 14, unreadable on older phones) replaced with a water wave and a cherry blossom (Unicode 6, on every phone); v35: directional arrow buttons (D-pad) in the garden and the hall — four small arrows (▲▼◀▶, old geometric glyphs, no emoji), shown only while the figure is walking and hidden when idle, the arrow matching the figure's current heading highlighted, hold to steer and release to stop; straight-line walking — the "go to the old tree" destination now stops right under the old pine instead of four units short, a collision dead zone that could leave a tap on a trunk centre circling for ever is fixed, and the garden camera is widened (FOV 60→66) and brought closer (follow 3.9→3.3) so a target grows clearly as you approach it; tree density — the courtyard trees are thinned from 39 to 34 (two centre-north outer trees and two of the five mid-ground trees behind the hall removed, one inner pine west of the path removed) with heights varied, so the tree line reads open and calm, not a dense wall.; v36: the hall is widened left-to-right (floor 21→25, side walls ±9.8→±11.8, ceiling and rafters widened to match) and the seats spread further — columns get the full +0.5 the visitor asked for (1.95→2.45, well over a metre of air between cushions), rows +0.25 (2.6→2.85, the most that fits: the front row is held by the worship burner / altar platform and the back row by the standing spot, so a full +0.5 front-to-back does not fit); the red carpet is narrowed (1.7→1.1) so it stays inside the centre aisle and never runs under a cushion; the colonnade is spread to match — pillars out to x=±10.2 and further apart front-to-back (3.6→4.2) — which also fixes a v33 latent bug where three pillar bases overlapped the outer cushions; camera and D-pad walk clamps widened so the figure can reach the outer seats. The doorway is now a real open entrance — two lacquered leaves swung open against the walls, a stone threshold, warmer light beyond — and a seated 'See the door' button (望門口) turns the gaze round to it, so you can watch people come in, then back to the altar; the hall's 24 frozen worshippers are replaced by three living ones who sit a while, rise, walk out through the doorway and come back in at their own pace, staggered and unhurried (the presence counter counts who is actually inside), and two more fellow visitors stroll the grounds on a slow loop, pausing to look around — so the temple feels lived in, never a room of frozen dummies; v37: two lamp posts removed from the entrance — the loose one at (-0.5, -2.4) stood dead on the stone-path centreline (0.01 off it), so the figure walked straight into it on the way to the gate, and the path-side one at z=-10.6 crowded the gate pair (only 1.35 from the left gate lantern); the gate pair stays flanking the steps and the path keeps five markers three paces off it, so the way in reads open and the walk from the courtyard to the gate is fully clear; the visitor figure no longer hides behind the bottom HUD — the garden camera aims lower (look point 1.15→-0.5) so the figure sits head-to-feet at 54–78% of the frame, clear of the guide chip, hint and buttons, and the bottom HUD is slimmed (guide portrait 48→40px, the redundant garden-note line removed, its text already in the guide chip) so the gap holds on short phones too.; stone benches — two became one: the single bench now sits alongside the stone path on its east side at (2.5, 0.4), long side parallel to the path, facing the pond across it, so the walkway is clear (the pond-side bench lay crosswise with its east edge only 0.64 off the path centreline, right beside the 'go to the lake' stopping point, and the old-pine bench lay across the direct walk to the old tree); D-pad arrows are bigger (56px buttons, 22px glyphs), every arrow now carries its word (前/後/左/右), the centre cell holds a needle that points the figure's live heading, a note under the pad says the arrows are relative to the camera, and walking backward (toward the camera, toward the viewer's face) gives the lit ▼ button a white ring on top of the gold, so the most confusing direction is unmistakable. Entry screen: the two acolyte portraits are spaced further apart (gap 18px → 40px), and the picker label is reworded — the visitor IS the acolyte, not choosing a companion, so '揀個小道童陪你入廟' becomes '你係邊個小道童？' (SC '你是哪个小道童？', EN 'Which little acolyte are you?'); the picker still sets the visitor's own figure, exactly as in v34.; v38: the D-pad now always sits above the 3D figure — on the visitor's phone the figure's head was covering the ◀左 button, because the arrow buttons were only 55% opaque (the dark figure showed straight through) and the stacking was left to chance; both canvas wraps now have an explicit z-index:0, the pad is raised to z-index:30, the buttons are near-opaque (88%) with a stronger border, the hint line under the pad gets a dark pill, and the pad is nudged 4px toward the right edge. Then the arrows were cut back to 48px (glyph 19px) — the middle ground between the 40px the visitor called too small and the 56px of v37 that overlapped the figure — and the pad became 8-way: four diagonal buttons (↖↗↙↘, 前左/前右/後左/後右） fill the corner cells, so the figure can walk any direction including forward-right; padVec/padQuad now work in 8 octants with unit diagonal vectors (no faster diagonals), the needle turns in 45-degree steps, and the backward white ring follows octant 4. Labels, needle and white ring otherwise exactly as in v37, in the garden and the hall alike.; v39: people no longer freeze in a bunch when they meet — the v36 "wait in place when blocked" rule is replaced by gentle collision avoidance: a walker whose path is blocked steers around the person ahead (a polite sidestep, the bend growing as the person gets closer and fading by 1.7 units), dead ahead is passed on the walker's own left so two walkers meeting head-on step to opposite sides and slip past each other, and nobody passes through anybody — a separation pass keeps a 0.55 minimum gap, walkers yield and the visitor is never shoved, seated figures stay put; the three hall worshippers also stop queueing in single file — each keeps its own lane down the aisle (offset ±0.55 from the carpet centreline) so a stopped figure ahead is walked around, not waited behind, and after 12 seconds with no progress a walker moves on to the next leg instead of circling for ever. The hall pillars can no longer fill the whole screen — the colonnade is slimmed (shaft .32/.38→.28/.34, collar .37→.33, capital .46→.42, base .95→.88) and the hall camera is pushed out to 2.5 units from every pillar axis, so a pillar close to the lens reads as a frame, not a wall. The doorway is now a real see-through opening with perspective — the flat glowing panel behind it is gone, replaced by the courtyard outside: sky and far mountains on a big painted plane (the same dusk view as the windows, 40 wide so it holds from every seat), sunlit grass and a stone path running out from the threshold, and real pines and a stone lantern standing in front of the painted view for true parallax. And the acolyte robes are one colour at last — the hall's three worshippers and the two garden strollers all wear the same dark slate-blue robe with pale gold trim as the visitor (v34 colour 0x46587e / 0xd6bd8a); the old mixed palette that left one worshipper brown and the others in two different blues is gone. The doorway is also made taller (opening 5.6→7.0, leaves 5.3→6.7, posts 5.6→7.0) — the old door read as too short against the 10.6 hall — and the painted sky plane grows to 40×20 so the taller opening's top sightline from the back row still lands on sky; the seated 'See the door' view is verified by projection: from every cushion where the doorway is in frame, the outside (painted sky and mountains, sunlit ground and path, pines and lantern) is visible through it — 16 of 40 cushions on a narrow portrait phone, 26 of 40 on a wider screen, all of them covered.; v40: the 8-way D-pad is replaced by a virtual joystick — drag it any way and the figure walks that way, true continuous 360° (the pad only ever gave eight fixed directions, and its arrows kept fighting the figure for the same patch of screen); the stick is one compact round control (120px) in the bottom-right corner, pinned above the 3D scene (z-index 30, near-opaque) so it can never be covered by the figure or show through; how far the knob is pushed sets the pace (a nudge is a slow stroll, a full push the normal pace); tap-to-walk on the ground works alongside it; the stick is always visible in the garden and appears in the hall whenever you are standing and free to walk (hidden while seated or mid-offering). The figure's heading is now read from the figure itself — it turns to face wherever it walks, the face and gold collar mark the front, the hair and the enlarged sash knot mark the back — so no direction arrows are needed.
- Deployed on Vercel (project `baibai`) with custom domains `baibai.hk` and `www.baibai.hk`.
