## Fortune sticks — full set of 100 (2026-11-15)

The fortune-drawing feature (求籤) now holds the complete set of 100 Wong Tai Sin sticks (#1–#100). The original 16 verified sticks (#1–#15 and #100) were joined by the remaining 84 (#16–#99), added in seven batches of 12, each verified verbatim against the official Sik Sik Yuen TaoNet pages (https://taonet.siksikyuen.org.hk/StickEnquiry/{number}/zh-TW) — title, poem (籤文), interpretation (解曰) and details (詳情) copied exactly, grades cross-checked against kaucim.ai (https://www.kaucim.ai/sign). Grade distribution matches the official source: Excellent (上上) 3, Very Good (上吉) 10, Good (中吉) 29, Fair (中平) 40, Poor (下下) 18. Each stick is stored trilingually (Traditional Chinese / Simplified Chinese / English); the new 84 also carry the full 詳情 detail pairs in the data. Two grades in the original 16 were corrected to the official TaoNet grades during the final batch: #7 (仁貴歸家) and #14 (東坡歸隱), both from Good (中吉) to Fair (中平). The in-app note now reads "一百支已齊" (the full 100 are in). No stick was fabricated or left out. Four clear typos in the official text were corrected on 2026-11-15 (per the visitor's rule to fix a wrong character when the correction is reasonable): #33 detail 迪→畜, #61 interpretation 幅→福, #89 interpretation 籤→劍, #96 verse 姜→羌 (and the matching English for #96, Jiang→Qiang flute). Variant characters (異體字） were left exactly as in the official text. v43 (2026-11-15) adds two more corrections under the same rule — #63 interpretation 貧亦無防防也→貧亦無防也 and #59 detail 失運勿莫動→失運莫動 — and makes the Traditional version fully Traditional Chinese: five titles corrected (#24 秦琼→秦瓊, #25 五岳→五嶽, #61 岳飛→嶽飛, #73/#75 倫文叙→倫文敘) plus a sweep of the remaining Simplified characters in the stick text (see the v43 note above), with the Simplified version already the correct conversion in every case so it needed no change, and the English unchanged where the meaning is the same. #100's title is corrected from 唐明皇賞花 to the official 百花開放 (TaoNet and kaucim.ai agree); #100 keeps its short modern note, so TaoNet's 永得此籤者 (itself a typo for 求得此籤者） has no text to correct in the app.

## Visitor stats — private (2026-11-15)

Vercel Web Analytics is enabled on the `baibai` project (added 2026-11-15). One script tag in `index.html` (`/_vercel/insights/script.js`). Stats are visible only in the Vercel dashboard (project baibai → Analytics): visitors per day, busiest times, countries/cities, referrers (Google / WhatsApp / direct / other sites), devices. Nothing is shown on the site itself — no public counter. No custom event tracking (what visitors do inside the temple is not recorded) and no personal data. Stats only accumulate from the moment analytics went live; earlier visits cannot be recovered.
