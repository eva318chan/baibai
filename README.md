# Baibai — online virtual temple demo

`baibai.hk` — 拜拜 線上虛擬廟宇 Demo (static site, v37).

- Static, no build step, no server, no secrets. Entry point: `index.html`.
- Assets live under `assets/` (Heart Sutra chant audio, logo badge, three.js).
- Public version was v26; this repo deploys v33 (v30 bigger garden + faster walking, v31 lake lotus/flowers/dragonflies/butterflies/koi, v32 entry wording, v33: incense — 3 sticks per visit + smoke only after offering + burner sized to the figure; leaving the hall goes straight back to the courtyard; map doubled, lake enlarged, pond life random, taller trees and mountains, temple pushed back for depth, hall seats widened, entry page logo cleaned up, temple plaque stays Chinese in every language; v34: the visitor is re-skinned as a little Taoist acolyte （道童） — dark-blue robe, pale-gold sash, topknot, chubbier head — with a boy/girl acolyte picker on the entry screen; the designed 道童 art is used as the picker portraits and as the sweeping acolyte who greets you in the garden; courtyard gate and hall differentiated — gate taller with a vermilion-red roof, hall pushed back to z -54, scaled 0.85, deep-green roof, so the near building reads clearly bigger and the far one smaller, and the hall roofline no longer cuts across the gate roof; lake and lotus buttons fixed — the U+1FAB7 lotus emoji (Unicode 14, unreadable on older phones) replaced with a water wave and a cherry blossom (Unicode 6, on every phone); v35: directional arrow buttons (D-pad) in the garden and the hall — four small arrows (▲▼◀▶, old geometric glyphs, no emoji), shown only while the figure is walking and hidden when idle, the arrow matching the figure's current heading highlighted, hold to steer and release to stop; straight-line walking — the "go to the old tree" destination now stops right under the old pine instead of four units short, a collision dead zone that could leave a tap on a trunk centre circling for ever is fixed, and the garden camera is widened (FOV 60→66) and brought closer (follow 3.9→3.3) so a target grows clearly as you approach it; tree density — the courtyard trees are thinned from 39 to 34 (two centre-north outer trees and two of the five mid-ground trees behind the hall removed, one inner pine west of the path removed) with heights varied, so the tree line reads open and calm, not a dense wall.; v36: the hall is widened left-to-right (floor 21→25, side walls ±9.8→±11.8, ceiling and rafters widened to match) and the seats spread further — columns get the full +0.5 the visitor asked for (1.95→2.45, well over a metre of air between cushions), rows +0.25 (2.6→2.85, the most that fits: the front row is held by the worship burner / altar platform and the back row by the standing spot, so a full +0.5 front-to-back does not fit); the red carpet is narrowed (1.7→1.1) so it stays inside the centre aisle and never runs under a cushion; the colonnade is spread to match — pillars out to x=±10.2 and further apart front-to-back (3.6→4.2) — which also fixes a v33 latent bug where three pillar bases overlapped the outer cushions; camera and D-pad walk clamps widened so the figure can reach the outer seats. The doorway is now a real open entrance — two lacquered leaves swung open against the walls, a stone threshold, warmer light beyond — and a seated 'See the door' button (望門口) turns the gaze round to it, so you can watch people come in, then back to the altar; the hall's 24 frozen worshippers are replaced by three living ones who sit a while, rise, walk out through the doorway and come back in at their own pace, staggered and unhurried (the presence counter counts who is actually inside), and two more fellow visitors stroll the grounds on a slow loop, pausing to look around — so the temple feels lived in, never a room of frozen dummies; v37: two lamp posts removed from the entrance — the loose one at (-0.5, -2.4) stood dead on the stone-path centreline (0.01 off it), so the figure walked straight into it on the way to the gate, and the path-side one at z=-10.6 crowded the gate pair (only 1.35 from the left gate lantern); the gate pair stays flanking the steps and the path keeps five markers three paces off it, so the way in reads open and the walk from the courtyard to the gate is fully clear; the visitor figure no longer hides behind the bottom HUD — the garden camera aims lower (look point 1.15→-0.5) so the figure sits head-to-feet at 54–78% of the frame, clear of the guide chip, hint and buttons, and the bottom HUD is slimmed (guide portrait 48→40px, the redundant garden-note line removed, its text already in the guide chip) so the gap holds on short phones too.; stone benches — two became one: the single bench now sits alongside the stone path on its east side at (2.5, 0.4), long side parallel to the path, facing the pond across it, so the walkway is clear (the pond-side bench lay crosswise with its east edge only 0.64 off the path centreline, right beside the 'go to the lake' stopping point, and the old-pine bench lay across the direct walk to the old tree); D-pad arrows are bigger (56px buttons, 22px glyphs), every arrow now carries its word (前/後/左/右), the centre cell holds a needle that points the figure's live heading, a note under the pad says the arrows are relative to the camera, and walking backward (toward the camera, toward the viewer's face) gives the lit ▼ button a white ring on top of the gold, so the most confusing direction is unmistakable. Entry screen: the two acolyte portraits are spaced further apart (gap 18px → 40px), and the picker label is reworded — the visitor IS the acolyte, not choosing a companion, so '揀個小道童陪你入廟' becomes '你係邊個小道童？' (SC '你是哪个小道童？', EN 'Which little acolyte are you?'); the picker still sets the visitor's own figure, exactly as in v34.
- Deployed on Vercel (project `baibai`) with custom domains `baibai.hk` and `www.baibai.hk`.
